    Export conversation history?

    The export starts now and may take a while. You can close this popup, but keep the ChatGPT tab open until it finishes.

    Optional: free up your ChatGPT history once your conversations are saved. Nothing is deleted before the export is complete.

    Deleting a project permanently removes all its chats, its project instructions, and the files stored only in that project. Instructions and project files are not included in this export. A project is kept if any of its conversations failed to export.

    How deletion works: 1) every selected conversation is exported first; 2) the export files are created and downloaded; 3) only then are the successfully exported conversations deleted. If you confirm here, you will not be asked again at the end. Conversations that fail to export are never deleted. Deleted conversations may take up to 10 minutes to disappear from your history.
