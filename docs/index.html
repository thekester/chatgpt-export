Currently supports ChatGPT in Firefox and Chromium-based browsers. Support for other AI platforms may be considered in the future; it is not available yet.

Built for your archive

Take your history with you.

Choose what to save

Export the open chat, selected conversations, or accessible history. Search and filter conversations before choosing.

Use formats that last

Save Markdown, HTML, or a JEX notebook for Joplin. ZIP exports can include media, attachments, metadata, and a searchable index.

Stay in control

Exports are assembled locally. Deletion is always a separate, explicit action; a deletion request may take up to 10 minutes to disappear from ChatGPT history.

Privacy comes first.

The extension does not send conversation data to its developer or an export server. It reads data from your signed-in ChatGPT session to create the files you request. Optional external-media access is only used when you choose to fetch images or files hosted outside ChatGPT.
